# Neural Cellular Automata (NCA) vs Von Neumann Networks (VNNs)

## Concept Overview
**Neural Cellular Automata (NCA)** blend Cellular Automata with Deep Learning. In NCAs, the discrete rules of traditional CAs are replaced by a Neural Network—specifically, a Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)—that resides inside *every single cell*. The MLP looks at the cell's local neighborhood and predicts the cell's next state. This approach is famous for morphogenesis, such as generating and regenerating images (textures, sprites) and 3D meshes.

## Advantages
- **Robustness**: Highly resilient to perturbations and damage; if you "erase" part of an image generated by an NCA, it can heal and regrow the missing parts.
- **Pattern Generation**: Excellent for complex texture and pattern generation.
- **Differentiable**: Fully differentiable, allowing the CA rules to be learned directly from data.

## Disadvantages
- **Computational Overhead**: Because a full MLP is executed at every single cell at every time step, NCAs are computationally very heavy and memory-intensive.
- **Black Box Rules**: The learned rules are embedded inside the MLP weights, making it difficult to extract simple, interpretative rules or minimal architectures.

## Comparison with VNNs
While both seek to learn cellular computation, **Von Neumann Networks (VNNs)** take a much more streamlined and fundamental approach to the cell itself.

- **Cellular Simplicity**: Instead of placing an entire MLP inside every cell, VNNs use a single, specialized "Von Neumann Neuron" in each cell. This neuron has a single learnable "Codd state" that dictates if it is a passive connection or an active non-linear switch.
- **Propagation via Green's Functions**: Instead of the cell's MLP processing neighbors, VNNs use global/local convolutions representing Green's functions to propagate signals across the field. 
- **Architecture Learning**: VNNs are designed to *become* the neural network architecture (like an MLP or ALU) embedded in the cellular space, whereas NCAs use MLPs to simulate CA rules. VNNs self-engineer their own macroscopic connectivity.
